About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Arlington. Since September 2025 I have been a member of the SMILE Lab, advised by Prof. Junzhou Huang.

My research focuses on multimodal machine learning for healthcare: combining clinical records, pathology, laboratory tests, diagnostic codes, and free-text reports to predict patient outcomes, and measuring how much each data source contributes for an individual patient. Before UTA, I was a visiting scholar in Mechanical Engineering at UC Berkeley, and I received my M.S. in Data Science from the University at Buffalo.
